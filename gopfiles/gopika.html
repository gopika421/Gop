Welcome to the mca department

Mohandas College of Engineering and Technology (MCET) offers a two-year full-time Master of Computer Applications (MCA) postgraduate program approved by AICTE and affiliated with APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University

  • Duration: 2 years (4 semesters)
  • Intake Capacity: 60seats
  • Eligibility: Graduation with at least 50% marks in Computer Science, Computer Applications, or a related discipline from a recognized university
  • Focus Areas: Software development, data analytics, artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, hardware, and networking
